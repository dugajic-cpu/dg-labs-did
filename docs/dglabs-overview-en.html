The Problem

KYC compliance is broken for fintechs

Swiss fintechs and crypto firms face strict FINMA identity verification obligations. Current solutions are fragmented, expensive, and create poor user experiences.

⚖️

FINMA requires strong KYC

Under the Anti-Money Laundering Act (AMLA) and FINMA circular 2016/7, Swiss financial intermediaries must verify the identity of their clients before establishing a business relationship. Digital-only onboarding requires identity assurance equivalent to in-person verification.

🔁

Users verify repeatedly

Every fintech runs its own KYC process. The same person submits their passport to Revolut, to a crypto exchange, to a bank — again and again. Each verification costs the company money and frustrates the user. There is no portable proof of verified identity.

🗄️

Centralised data creates risk

Traditional KYC stores copies of passports and personal data in company databases. Each copy is a liability — a breach target, a GDPR obligation, a storage cost. The more copies exist, the greater the risk surface.

💸

Verification costs add up

Per-verification costs from KYC providers range from $0.80 to $5 per check. For high-volume onboarding, this becomes a significant operational expense — especially when users abandon mid-process and the cost is still incurred.

The Solution

Verifiable credentials: verify once, present anywhere

DG Labs has built a pipeline that issues a W3C-standard Verifiable Credential after biometric verification. The credential lives in the user's Microsoft Authenticator wallet — portable, privacy-preserving, and cryptographically verifiable by any relying party.

✓

One verification, multiple uses

Once issued, the credential can be presented to any organisation that accepts it — without repeating the biometric process.

✓

No data stored by relying parties

The verifier receives a cryptographic proof, not a copy of the passport. Nothing is stored. GDPR obligations are minimised.

✓

User-controlled identity

The credential lives in the user's wallet. They decide who to share it with and what attributes to disclose.

✓

Cryptographically tamper-proof

Credentials are signed with DG Labs' DID key (did:web:www.dgajic.com). Any forgery attempt is immediately detectable.

✓

Standards-based

Built on W3C Verifiable Credentials, DID:web, and Microsoft Entra Verified ID — open standards with broad ecosystem support.

✓

Production-ready today

Live pipeline, tested with real external users, biometric verification via Veriff Essential (Full Auto AI), credential in Microsoft Authenticator.

Compliance

Aligned with Swiss and EU regulatory requirements

The DG Labs pipeline is designed with FINMA and EU regulatory frameworks as primary constraints, not afterthoughts.

Swiss regulatory alignment

  • FINMA AML/KYC — biometric passport + liveness check satisfies strong identity assurance
  • AMLA Art. 3 — digital identity verification equivalent to in-person
  • FINMA Circular 2016/7 — video identification via accredited provider (Veriff)
  • nLPD (Swiss FADP) — minimal data retention, user consent, privacy by design
  • FINMA Crypto licensing — KYC obligation for DLT trading facilities and crypto exchanges

EU regulatory alignment

  • eIDAS 2.0 — W3C Verifiable Credentials align with EUDIW architecture
  • GDPR — no personal data stored by verifier, data minimisation principle
  • EU AI Act — biometric verification system risk classification considered
  • MiCA — crypto-asset service provider KYC obligation alignment
  • AML6D — enhanced due diligence capability via credential attributes
